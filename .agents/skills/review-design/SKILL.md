---
name: review-design
description: Review a given design document for completeness, correctness, and quality. Use before handing the design to the user for final review.
---

Should be executed by the `design-reviewer` subagent.

## Rules

- Make sure the design does not introduce any unnecessary complexity.
- Validate the correctness of the proposed architecture and technical decisions.
- Ensure the design follows the industry best practices as of the current year.
- Ensure the design template (`docs/templates/design.md`) format and rules are
  followed. You can use previous design documents under `docs/design` as style
  exemplars.
